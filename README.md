### [Volentera — Hospital Volunteer Training Platform](https://github.com/AdarshS15/Volentera)

**Live Application:** [volentera.com](https://volentera.com)

### [Predictive Modeling of Cellular States Using scRNA-seq](https://github.com/AdarshS15/scRNA-seq-Fibroblast-Senescence-EMT-Forecasting)

Independent computational biology research investigating the prediction of future cellular states using time-course single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) data.

- Developed and benchmarked six machine learning architectures: Ridge Regression, Multilayer Perceptron, Random Forest, XGBoost, Neural ODE, and Temporal-Token Transformer.
- Compared Sinkhorn optimal-transport-based cell pairing with randomized pairing strategies.
- Investigated cellular-state forecasting in fibroblast senescence and epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT).
- Applied PCA-based dimensionality reduction and training-only preprocessing to high-dimensional gene-expression data.
- Evaluated model performance using R², Wasserstein-2 distance, endpoint prediction accuracy, and population variance preservation.
- Completed a first-author research manuscript documenting the experimental methodology and findings.

**Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, XGBoost, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, PCA, Optimal Transport
